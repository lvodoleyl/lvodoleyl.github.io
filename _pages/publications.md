## Research Interests

My research focuses on **Music Information Retrieval (MIR)**. Specifically, I am interested in:

- **Representation Learning**: Developing effective representations for complex data
- **Metric Learning**: Learning distance metrics for complex data
- **Music Signal Processing**: Processing and analyzing audio signals
- **Computational Linguistics**: NLP methods for texts and music
- **Speech Technology**: ASR, TTS, and speech processing systems
